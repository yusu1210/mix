# Code of Conduct

Mix is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat users and contributors with respect, regardless of background,
  identity, experience, or point of view.
- Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and respect stated privacy
  and security boundaries.
- Remove credentials, account identifiers, private paths, and transcript
  content from every public report.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when needed to protect the community. If you experience or witness
unacceptable behavior, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method
on their GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue for a report that identifies
an affected person without their consent.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as maintainer capacity
allows. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is
not acceptable.

This code applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
release channels, and other spaces where someone represents Mix.
